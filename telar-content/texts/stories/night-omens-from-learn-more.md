In this omen the left man represents a courageous warrior that has experienced warfare. They would essentially be the complete opposite of cowards who would crawl in their fours away frantically. These warriors would then follow the knocking sound until finding out what was giving out the sound. From this, the warrior would brawl all over the place with the object making the sound and grab it to demand what it is. These men wore vibrant capes and held dominance against evil omens.
(Florentine Codex, bk. 5, Ch.3, fol. 5r. Los Angeles: Getty Research Institute, 2023. Accessed 23 August 2026.)

The object or the man on the left would be a headless man that represent an omen named yohualtepuztli, which translates to "night ax." They resemble a ghost that knocks at night which sounds like someone cutting wood. The ghost occurs only at night's first slumber as everyone would be fast asleep. This then scares the cowardly as there would be no other sounds playing but just the ghosts.
(Florentine Codex, bk. 5, Ch.3, fol. 4v. Los Angeles: Getty Research Institute, 2023. Accessed 23 August 2026.)
