---
title: "Response to the Omen"
---

In the Florentine Codex, it is understood that you can take action against the owl's warning. After hearing the owl's song, the person would go to an omen interpreter (or soothsayer) who would guide them on what to do next. Showcasing that omens were not only predictions of possible misfortune, but they could also influence how people respond to uncertainty. The interpreter helped turn the meaning of the omen into guidance, perhaps to fight off the misfortune of fate. ( Florentine Codex, Book 5, fol.7r)
