It was said that this little person would only appear at night when young men wished to use the pee in the latrines, which were often heaps of dung. For this omen to be revealed someone was to be marked for death. The brave men who chose to chase this little person were often left disappointed and defrauded. Once they began the pursuit she would disappear only to reappear further away.
