---
title: "The Nahua Family"
---

The Nahua artstyle had special conventions that allowed for clearmarratives to be constructed. They had gendered clothing and haircuts. The size of each element would also be relevant. There are two boys stting down (one of them being bigger than the other), and a woman standing up. We can infer that this woman is their mom, ad the boys are meant to be siblings.
