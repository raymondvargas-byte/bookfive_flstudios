---
title: "The Purpose of the Fifth Book"
---

The purpose of the *Florentine Codex* was to have a complete encyclopedic record of the rapidly vanishing Nahua culture, history, and natural history. However, the fifth book is extremely precise about the intent in which these omens and prognostications were being recorded. The last paragraph of the fifth book reads as: 

These superstitions are an impediment to the faith, and this is why it is good to know them and preach against them. Only these few have been presented \[here\], even though there are many more. Diligent preachers and confessors should seek them out in order to pay attention to them during confession and to preach against them, for they are like a mange that sickens the faith (Garcia Garagarza 2023)
