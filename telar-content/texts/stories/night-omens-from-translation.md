The General History of the Things of New Spain, better known as the Florentine Codex, is the magnum opus of el Colegio Imperial de la Santa Cruz of Tlatelolco, Mexico’s first college. A Spanish Franciscan friar and a group of Nahua scholars and artists came together to create an encyclopedia that included all aspects of Indigenous knowledge and the customs and history of central Mexico.
