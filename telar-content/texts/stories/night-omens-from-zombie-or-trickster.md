---
title: "Zombie or Trickster God? Both?"
---

At nighttime the dead were said to appear moaning and in their funeral wrappings. If you were valiant enough to approach and grasp at this dead person, you'd only end up with a lump of grass in your hand. Some said this omen was **Tezcatlipoca** in disguise. Tezcatlipoca, a central God in Nahua religion, disguise himself as a dead person to mock men in the mortal world.
